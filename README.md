# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Using SQL

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **e-commerce sales, customer, order, and product data using SQL**. The goal is to identify revenue trends, top-performing products, customer behavior, and key business opportunities.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall and monthly revenue
* Identify top-selling products and categories
* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Identify repeat and high-value customers
* Analyze cancellations and customer churn
* Generate business insights and recommendations

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **MySQL**
* **SQL**
* **CTEs**
* **Window Functions**
* **Aggregate Functions**
* **Joins & Subqueries**

## 📊 Key Insights

* Total revenue: **₹39.55M**
* Electronics contributed **63.77%** of total revenue.
* **Smartphone X12** was the highest-revenue product.
* **80 customers** were identified as repeat customers.
* Customer churn rate was **5%**.
* Order cancellation rate was **12%**.
* **June 2026** recorded the highest monthly revenue at **₹5.17M**.

## 💡 Main Business Recommendations

* Focus on high-performing **Electronics** products and maintain sufficient inventory.
* Promote high-revenue products such as **Smartphone X12**.
* Use loyalty programs to retain high-value and repeat customers.
* Reduce cancellations by identifying issues with inventory, payment, and delivery.
* Use personalized offers and recommendations to reduce customer churn.
* Create product bundles and cross-selling strategies.
